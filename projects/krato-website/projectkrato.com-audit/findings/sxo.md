# Search Experience Optimization (SXO) — projectkrato.com

Qualitative only — not part of the weighted health score.

## Page-type match

The homepage reads as a **commercial landing page** (single service business, clear CTA, guarantee, process). For the terms it's realistically positioned to catch early ("sistemas de venta con IA para pymes México", "software a medida CDMX"), searchers are mixing:

- **Commercial-investigation intent** ("¿qué es un CRM con IA?", "agencia de automatización CDMX") — served reasonably well by the KRATO method explainer and service breakdown.
- **Transactional intent** ("contratar agencia IA ventas México") — served well by the guarantee section and contact form.

No mismatch detected — the page type fits the intent it's realistically capturing today. The risk isn't page-type mismatch, it's **topical thinness** (see `content.md`): a single page is being asked to satisfy both intents for several different services at once.

## User stories this page currently serves

1. "I run a PYME, my leads go cold, I want to know if this company can fix it" — served directly by the hero + guarantee.
2. "I want to know exactly what they'd build for me" — served by the two service cards, though at a fairly high level of abstraction (no example deliverables, screenshots, or mockups).
3. "I want to know if this is legitimate / who's behind it" — **not well served yet** — this is the same E-E-A-T gap flagged in `content.md`.
