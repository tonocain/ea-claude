# Project Krato — Avatar & Offer

Internal working document. Source of truth for who Project Krato targets and what's sold.

## Part 1 — The Avatar

**Who:** Owners/directors of PYMEs (small/medium businesses). Local businesses and small/medium shops that need to streamline heavy operational processes. Sweet spot: small enough to automate without big-company complications.

**Industries:** Professional services (lawyers, consultants), private healthcare (dermatologists, dentists, gynecologists), restaurants/fast food/delivery, and B2B agencies.

**Urgent problem:** The business can grow, but operations lag and keep it "boxed in." Every new client or service gets slower because of manual tasks and lack of attention. Repetitive reports get generated by hand when they could be automated — time that should go to real-value work. They know this costs money and time and slows growth. There are also critical dependencies in their workflows (a single person or a single manual process) that represent a major risk: if it fails, operations can stop, and they don't know how to fix it.

**What they've tried that didn't work:**
- Improvised Excel/Sheets that end in chaos
- Hiring more staff to absorb repetitive tasks (instead of eliminating them)
- Generic tools (template CRM/chatbots) that don't adapt to their real workflow

**Dream outcome:** A measurable headline number (e.g. save 10-15h/week, or reduce report-generation time by X%), backed by a qualitative benefit: operating without depending on a single person or manual process that could stop the business, and being able to grow or take on more clients without operations collapsing.

**Where they are:** LinkedIn. Google Maps, local businesses with visible phone numbers. Corporate website with a contact email. Referrals and trusted recommendations, especially in professional services and private healthcare. Email scraping via Google Maps and websites as the main prospecting channel.

## Part 2 — The Offer

**What's delivered:** Process diagnostic — BPMN mapping of critical workflows across four areas: **Sales, Marketing, Delivery, Administration.**
- Identification of bottlenecks, automatable repetitive tasks, and risk dependencies in each area.
- Prioritized roadmap: what to automate first (with or without AI/automation tools), and expected ROI.
- Results presentation session with the owner/director.

**Guaranteed outcome:** At the end of the diagnostic, the client gets detailed knowledge of their processes and a clear implementation roadmap. Once implemented (by Project Krato or a third party), they can save time and resources.

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for the full diagnostic.
