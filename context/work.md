# Work / Business

**Company:** Project Krato (projectkrato.com)

**What we do:** AI sales systems + custom software for SMBs in Mexico. Positioning: businesses don't have a lead problem, they have a system problem — leads go cold from slow, disorganized response, not lack of demand.

**Service lines:**
1. **AI Sales & Intelligence Systems** — centralized CRM, 24/7 response, automatic qualification, voice agents.
2. **Custom Software** — management dashboards, ops automation, AI reporting, integrated agents.

**Methodology — KRATO method:**
- **K**now — full business audit + Krato Score (0–100): site, presence, conversion, competition.
- **R**oadmap — define what gets built first, in what order, with what success metric.
- **A**utomate — build the system (CRM, AI agents, automations, custom software).
- **T**rigger — phased launch, activated with real usage data.
- **O**ptimize — 30 days of post-launch optimization on real data.

**Guarantee:** "It works, or we keep working for free." No public pricing — price and scope are defined after the free diagnostic (the Know phase).

**Revenue:** Pre-revenue — no clients/revenue yet. Finding first clients is the current #1 business priority.

**Tools:** CRM in Airtable (migration to Twenty planned), n8n, Miro, Claude Code.

**MCP servers connected:** Miro, Gmail, n8n, trigger.dev. Comfortable setting up any additional MCP server as needed.
